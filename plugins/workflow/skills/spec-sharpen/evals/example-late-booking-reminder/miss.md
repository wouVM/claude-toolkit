EXAMPLE CASE: invented to show the format. Not a real ticket.

Bookings made less than 24 hours before they start never got a reminder. The reminder job picked
up bookings as they crossed the 24-hour mark, so a booking made at 15:00 for 19:00 the same day was
never selected. Those same-day bookings were the no-shows the venue had complained about. Proof:
the client re-test two weeks after release, a booking made at 14:10 for 19:30 that day, no reminder
sent, and the job log shows the booking was never picked up.
