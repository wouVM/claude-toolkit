# Booking reminders

## Why
Guests who forget their booking leave tables empty. A reminder cuts no-shows.

## Acceptance criteria
1. A guest receives a reminder message 24 hours before their booking starts.
2. The reminder shows the date, time and party size, and a way to cancel.
3. A guest who cancels from the reminder frees the table in the booking list.

## Out of scope
Reminders by text message; e-mail only for now.
